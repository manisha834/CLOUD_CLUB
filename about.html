About Us

From a handful of cloud enthusiasts to a thriving 500-member community.

Our Story

Founded in 2019 by a small group of computer science students passionate about distributed systems, the Cloud Computing Club has grown into one of the largest tech communities on campus.

We bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry practice through workshops, certifications, hackathons, and partnerships with leading cloud providers.

Whether you are deploying your first S3 bucket or architecting multi-region Kubernetes clusters, there is a place for you here.

What We Do

Educate

Weekly workshops on cloud platforms, DevOps tooling, and architecture patterns.

Connect

Bring students, alumni, and recruiters together through networking events.

Innovate

Host hackathons and capstone projects that solve real-world problems.
