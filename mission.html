Mission & Vision

Why we exist — and where we are headed.

Mission

To empower every student with practical cloud-computing skills, regardless of major or background, through accessible learning and a supportive community.

Vision

To be the leading student cloud community in the region — producing graduates who shape the future of cloud-native engineering.

Values

Curiosity, collaboration, inclusivity, and continuous learning guide every workshop, event, and decision we make.

Strategic Goals 2026

Reach 1,000 Members

Expand to satellite campuses and online communities.

200 Certifications

Industry Pipeline

Place 50+ members in cloud internships through partnerships.
